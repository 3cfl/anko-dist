anko respects the intellectual property rights of others. if you believe that content available on this website infringes your copyright, you may submit a dmca takedown request.

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your full name and contact information (email address is required).
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL(s) or game(s) on anko that you believe infringe your copyright.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full legal name is acceptable for electronic submissions).
